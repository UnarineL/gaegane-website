Northwest Farmhouse

A refined modern farmhouse aesthetic shaped around landscape, function and timeless design.

The Northwest Farmhouse project involved the architectural and interior architectural enhancement of an existing farmhouse situated on a 6.8-hectare working farm. Drawing inspiration from the surrounding landscape and mining character of the region, the design embraces raw natural textures, durable materials and clean architectural lines to create a refined modern farmhouse aesthetic.

The existing structure was reconfigured into a functional three-bedroom open-plan residence tailored for contemporary farm living, while still supporting the operational needs of the working farm. The result is a cohesive architectural and interior environment that balances practicality, comfort and timeless design integrity.
