Our Story

Gaegane Construction Group was founded with a focus on becoming a turnkey construction and development company. Although the company initially specialised in maintenance and small-scale construction services, it has since grown into a multidisciplinary construction group specialising in high end residential and commercial projects, managing every stage from architectural planning and interior design coordination to construction and final handover.

From the beginning, our approach has never been about simply building structures, but about creating spaces that families are proud to live in, developments that stand the test of time, and projects executed with a level of care that clients can genuinely feel throughout the process.

Today, we work alongside a highly skilled network of architects, engineers, artisans, technicians, and supporting staff who share our commitment to quality craftsmanship, functionality, and refined design. This collaborative approach allows us to provide clients with a seamless building experience while maintaining the perfect balance between aesthetics, function, and cost. Focused on delivering infrastructure solutions that contribute to the development of communities, we aim to enhance lives by creating opportunities through infrastructure development. At our core, we believe construction is about more than buildings, it is about creating environments that enhance the way people live, work, and experience the world around them.

B-BBEE & Transformation

We are committed to a Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE), with a comprehensive strategy that aims to achieve meaningful representation of black people at ownership, senior executive, and employment level.

Continuously empowering people through practical, onsite training, and providing employment opportunities to people living in the surrounding areas of our sites.

We are a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor.
