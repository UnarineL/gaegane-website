About Us

Gaegane Construction Group

Gaegane Construction Group was founded with a focus of providing a Turnkey Solution from Architecture, Interior Design to Construction.

We work with a highly skilled team of architects, artisans, engineers, technicians and supporting staff which ensure that we provide the perfect balance between aesthetics, function and cost.

Focused on providing infrastructure solutions for the development of communities in Africa, enhancing lives by creating opportunities through infrastructure development. We pride ourselves in providing cost-effective solutions which benefit both the client and stakeholder.

B-BBEE & Transformation

We are committed to a Broad-Based Black Economic Empowerment (B-BBEE), with a comprehensive strategy that aims to achieve meaningful representation of black people at ownership, senior executive, and employment level. Continuously empowering people through practical, onsite training, and providing employment opportunities to people living in the surrounding areas of our sites.

We are a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor.
