Heritage Cottage

Architectural redesign, interior design and construction of an existing heritage cottage.

This renovation project involved the architectural redesign, interior design and construction of an existing heritage cottage in Bryanston. Drawing inspiration from the mid-century character and history on which Bryanston was founded, the design introduced internal alterations to improve flow and create a modern open-plan living environment, while carefully preserving the original charm and heritage of the cottage. The interior design approach focused on developing a cohesive and refined spatial experience, enhancing the sense of arrival and movement throughout the home while creating warm, timeless and functional living spaces.
